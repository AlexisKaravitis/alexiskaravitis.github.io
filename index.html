Design of a Fatigue Testing Machine for Elastomer Life Characterization

A purpose-built rig for measuring the fatigue life of elastomers under cyclic loading, designed end to end from dynamics to manufacturable hardware.

Designing a fatigue testing rig for elastomer specimens, from dynamic model through to a sized, manufacturable design.

It combines Simulink dynamic modelling, CATIA V5 CAD and FEA-based component sizing in one workflow. Elastomers govern ride, NVH and durability across tyres, bushings, mounts and dampers, and they're hard to model, which makes their fatigue life one of the trickier problems in vehicle development.

Simulink CATIA V5 FEA Dynamic Modelling Fatigue Analysis Elastomers Test Rig Design

// BENG THESIS · UNIWA

Identification of Constitutive Material Parameters in AM Structures using AI

An FEA and machine-learning workflow for predicting the properties of 3D-printed laminates.

An AI-optimisation workflow that predicts 3D-printed material behaviour and cuts physical testing.

Built an LSTM model in MATLAB and used Genetic Algorithms to optimise the FEA, so machine learning, simulation and material science ran as one pipeline. Modelled FDM layers as orthotropic plies under classical laminate theory to capture the anisotropy, then predicted stress–strain response. The workflow replaced much of the physical testing for a hard-to-model material class.

MATLAB LSTM Genetic Algorithms FEA Classical Laminate Theory FDM DoE
